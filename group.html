Join the lab

I'm looking for students who enjoy both working with real field data and writing code to explain it.

What you would work on

  • Interpreting fiber-optic data from geothermal, CO2 storage and mining sites
  • Building coupled flow, geomechanics and reactive transport models
  • Machine learning methods that connect measurements and models

Helpful background

  • Geology, geophysics, or petroleum, civil, environmental or mechanical engineering
  • Some programming, for example Python or MATLAB
  • Interest in numerical methods, signal processing or data science

You don't need all of these. Curiosity and persistence matter more.

Graduate students

Prospective M.S. and Ph.D. students apply through the Department of Environmental Engineering and Earth Sciences (EEES) graduate program. Before applying, email me your CV, unofficial transcripts, and a few sentences on which part of this research interests you.

Clemson undergraduates

Undergraduates in geology, engineering and related majors are welcome. Projects are real research, with the goal of presenting or publishing the results.
